## Key decisions
- Season definition: CREA/Kumar et al. 4-season scheme (summer Mar-May, monsoon Jun-Sep, post_monsoon Oct-Nov, winter Dec-Feb), chosen for alignment with the airshed literature the project builds on, over the STMEM paper's Jan-Mar winter definition
- Study window changed from calendar year 2025 to a rolling Mar 2025-Feb 2026 window, to capture one complete winter season. Driven by an OpenAQ network-wide sensor-registration discontinuity starting 2025-02-19 affecting nearly all Delhi stations — confirmed independently by checking the OpenAQ website directly, not just the API
- MIN_HOURS = 2 (not 1) for daily aggregation: a 1-hour "average" gives no noise protection and doesn't guarantee coverage of both MAIAC overpasses (Terra ~10:30, Aqua ~13:30)
- Station 11607 kept despite partial winter coverage (sensor stopped reporting 2025-12-27): 3 of 4 seasons strong, no stuck/zero problems found, judged not worth losing ~7500 good rows over one thin season
- Correlation check's purpose clarified: testing whether physically close stations' ground-truth PM2.5 still move together over time (a sanity check on coordinate/instrument correctness), not a multicollinearity concern for the LME — the model's station random intercept already absorbs constant offsets between stations sharing an AOD pixel
- `final_filter.py`'s status-column-based filtering established as the single source of truth for station inclusion going forward, replacing the params.yaml `drop_stations` list approach, to prevent the two lists drifting out of sync again
