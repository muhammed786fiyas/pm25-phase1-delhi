Verified the pooled/aggregate numbers were unaffected by the bug (pooling across all folds reconstructs the full dataset's group memberships either way) -- only the per-fold table was actually wrong before the fix.

## Key decisions

- **LandUse bucket in the equation = WorldCover + NDVI + terrain + OSM covariates** (everything non-AOD/non-season/non-met), since the project context doc's equation only names "Met" and "LandUse" as broad groups with no finer split specified.
- **Within-R2 group means computed from the full dataset being validated, not per-fold** -- necessary for the 42-fold random CV (tiny per-fold groups), applied consistently to spatial LOSO too so both schemes use the same reference.
- **CV fold fits use REML=True, same as the primary reported model** -- consistency over switching to ML for the CV folds; predictive R2/RMSE/MAE don't require ML vs REML the way nested-model likelihood comparisons would.
- **Random-CV prediction uses fixed effects + the fitted random intercept (when available) for that fold**, spatial-LOSO prediction uses fixed effects only -- this is what actually produces the "memorized per-station baseline" leakage effect Muhammed described, not just the different fold split.
- **Gap-fill robustness check re-runs spatial LOSO only, not random CV** -- matches the brief ("run the primary spatial-CV validation twice"), logged to its own separate MLflow experiment as explicitly requested.
- **Naive exp() back-transform reported as a secondary, clearly-labeled metric** (not the primary one) -- primary metrics stay on the `modeling_target` (log-PM2.5) scale the model actually fits/validates on; proper bias-corrected back-transformation (Duan's smearing) stays a pending item, unchanged from DAY8.

## Follow-up (later same day): outlier investigation + within-R2 formula switch

**Outlier investigation (5598, 6934) -- resolved.** Pulled each station's land-use covariates and compared them to the other 41 stations' distributions, plus checked their fitted random intercepts. Both sit at genuine extremes of the land-use covariate space -- station 6934 (Karni Singh Shooting Range)'s `shrubland_pct` (5.88) and `road_density_km_per_km2` (-2.11) both fall literally outside the range spanned by the other 41 stations; station 5598 (Sector-125, Noida)'s `elevation_m` (-1.44) is likewise below every other station's minimum, with `industrial_landuse_fraction` at the boundary. Under LOSO, holding either out forces the model to extrapolate its fitted linear land-use coefficients into territory it never saw in training -- the classic linear-model failure mode. Ruled out an oversized station-specific baseline (both stations' fitted random intercepts sit at unremarkable 31st/67th percentiles among all 42 stations) and already-ruled-out the 2km buffer (0 excluded neighbors for both). Not a data-quality bug -- a genuine extrapolation/coverage gap in the station network.

**Within-R2 formula switched to Kawano et al.'s literal definition, replacing the original shared-demeaning version** (explicit request). The two formulas were compared on our own spatial-LOSO output first and shown to swing from -0.335 to +0.211 on identical predictions, purely from the demeaning-method choice. `compute_within_r2()` (both true and predicted demeaned by the same true group mean, classic R2) removed entirely; replaced with `compute_within_r2_kawano()` (true demeaned by its own group's true mean, predicted demeaned by its own group's *predicted* mean -- computed once from the full pooled out-of-fold prediction set, same instability-avoidance reasoning as the earlier group-means fix -- then squared Pearson correlation of the two independently-demeaned series). Required restructuring `run_spatial_loso_cv`/`run_random_cv` into two passes (collect every fold's OOF predictions first, then compute the predicted-side group means and within-R2 second), since the predicted group mean isn't knowable until every fold has run. Re-ran all 3 validation pieces; deleted the 3 stale pre-switch MLflow runs (old formula, same run names) so only the corrected runs remain, and added a `within_r2_formula` MLflow param to every run going forward for traceability.

Updated headline numbers (R2/RMSE/MAE unchanged, only within-R2 changed):
- Spatial LOSO (primary): R2 = 0.423, within-R2 = **0.211** (was -0.335), RMSE = 0.638, MAE = 0.453.
- Random CV: R2 = 0.634, within-R2 = **0.206** (was 0.152), RMSE = 0.509, MAE = 0.375.
- Gap-fill robustness (excl. low confidence): R2 = 0.429, within-R2 = **0.189** (was -0.443), RMSE = 0.632, MAE = 0.442.
- Under the Kawano formula the spatial-LOSO vs random-CV within-R2 gap almost disappears (0.211 vs 0.206) even though the overall R2 gap stays large (0.423 vs 0.634) -- consistent with the leakage effect being mostly a per-station *level*-calibration artifact (which independent demeaning specifically cancels out), not a difference in how well either scheme tracks day-to-day anomalies.
- The two outlier stations no longer look anomalous on within-R2 specifically: station 5598's within-R2 = 0.190 and station 6934's = 0.235 (both in the normal range across folds), even though their plain R2 is still severely negative (-3.43, -1.44). The old shared-demeaning within-R2 punished their level-miscalibration directly (that's what dragged the pooled -0.335 negative); Kawano's independent demeaning cancels that same level offset out.
