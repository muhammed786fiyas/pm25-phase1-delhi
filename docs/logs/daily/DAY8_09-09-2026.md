## Key decisions
- **Season-intercept promoted over full season-specific fit** -- season-intercept captured nearly all of full season-specific fit's CV accuracy (overall LOOCV RMSE 0.267 vs 0.266) without the small-sample monsoon slope fragility (full season-specific fit uses only ~19 monsoon days per station). Full comparison in `data/interim/maiac_gapfill/calibration_variant_comparison.csv`.
- **Static-covariate-augmented pooled model rejected** -- for the 38-station shared-MERRA2-cell cluster, adding NDVI/land-cover/road-density/industrial-fraction/elevation as extra regressors gave essentially no CV improvement over MERRA2-only (0.2878 vs 0.2875 RMSE).
- **Confidence scoring uses leave-one-out CV on the pooled per-station model, grouped by season only for reporting** -- not a separate model fit per season -- so confidence describes how well the one real production model performs in each season.
- **Confidence merged into the main gap-filled output, not left as a separate lookup table** -- `confidence_bucket`/`confidence_rmse` populated for filled rows, `"observed"`/null for real MAIAC readings, so downstream modeling doesn't need a manual join.
- **Confidence bucket thresholds are empirical terciles of the RMSE distribution**, not fixed AOD-unit guesses, and get re-derived any time the underlying calibration model changes.
- Clarified two different downstream uses for the two confidence representations: `confidence_bucket` for an LME sensitivity analysis (refit excluding low-confidence filled days), raw `confidence_rmse` as a LightGBM feature (trees can find their own thresholds/interactions, so hand-picked cutoffs are less useful there than for a linear model).
- **WorldCover collinearity reduction differs by model, confirmed with Muhammed**: LME drops to 6 columns (3 always-zero + `wetland_herbaceous_pct` dropped for having no information, `water_pct` dropped as the reference category to resolve the compositional-data collinearity). LightGBM keeps 8 columns (only drops the 3 always-zero ones) -- explicitly decided *against* matching LME's 6 for LightGBM, since `water_pct` has real station-to-station variation (nonzero at 55% of stations) and trees pay no collinearity cost for keeping it.
- **Confidence/gap-fill metadata stays out of the base LME regression** -- `confidence_bucket` is carried as a non-regressor column for a future sensitivity-analysis script, not fit directly into the base LME model.
- **The 113 unfillable AOD station-days are not special-cased** -- LME drops them via ordinary complete-case filtering (only 101 of the 113 overlap with a PM2.5 reading), LightGBM keeps them with native NaN in `aod_055`, consistent with the general NaN-handling split already decided for the two model types.

- **`target_transform = log` for the LME dataset** -- decided from Section 9's residual diagnostics (fitted MixedLM on both candidates), not from the raw target's skew: raw-scale residual variance blows up ~12.8x from low to high fitted values, log-scale stays flat. The `NOT_SET` guard enforced that the EDA notebook was actually reviewed before this could be set.
- **WorldCover VIF stays unresolved by further grouping** -- `built_up_pct`/`tree_cover_pct`/`cropland_pct` remain elevated (VIF 160/96/65) even after the reference-category drop, but grouping them would erase `cropland_pct`'s distinct crop-burning signal; reported as a known limitation instead (widens coefficient SEs, doesn't invalidate the model).
- **The 46 zero-PM2.5 and 11 negative-AOD fixes apply to both prepared datasets**, not just LME -- these are data-quality issues (wrong label, impossible reading) independent of model type, unlike the LME-specific log-transform.
