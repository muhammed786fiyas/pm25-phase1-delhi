**Study design decisions**
- Study window: March 1, 2025 – February 28, 2026 (full 12-month seasonal cycle), chosen to avoid the OpenAQ CPCB network-wide data discontinuity before Feb 19, 2025
- Season labeling: 4-season CREA/Kumar et al. scheme — summer (Mar–May), monsoon (Jun–Sep), post-monsoon (Oct–Nov), winter (Dec–Feb)

## Data notes & gotchas
- `datetime_from`/`datetime_to` API parameters work; `date_from`/`date_to` are silently ignored.
- Network-wide CPCB sensor registration discontinuity starts 2025-02-19 on OpenAQ, regardless of a station's actual history — several NCR stations show `datetime_first = 2025-02-19`, which is an ingestion batch date shared across 5 stations, **not** true station commissioning. Caps their max possible 2025 coverage around 86%; CPCB may hold earlier data this pull doesn't return.
- Station 11607 (Lodhi Road, IITM) has thin winter data — stops Dec 27, 2025.
- Near-duplicate pixel pairs flagged in `shares_maiac_pixel_risk`: 10900/10921 (0.59 km apart), 5634/11607 (0.67 km apart) — likely to land in the same MAIAC 1km pixel.
- Borderline-distance pairs also worth watching: 5404/5630/5610 cluster, 5634/6957.
- Station 5610 (labeled "North Campus, DU" in OpenAQ) is physically located in Patel Nagar per the CPCB CCR dashboard — name is misleading, coordinate accepted as-is after verification.
- `rows_per_station_per_season.csv` (per-station row counts by season) shows several stations with severe seasonal gaps, not just low overall completeness: station 301 has 0 rows in summer and monsoon; 6936 has 0 summer, 42 monsoon; 10820, 10825, 10900 are thin across multiple seasons. These are strong candidates for the completeness-threshold drop.
