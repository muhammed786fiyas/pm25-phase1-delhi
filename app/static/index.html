Delhi PM2.5 Estimator

Satellite-to-ground calibration, Phase 1 — LightGBM on MAIAC AOD, ERA5 meteorology and land-use covariates.

Retrospective, not live. This estimates PM2.5 for a past date in the study window (2025-03-01 to 2026-02-28). It cannot report current air quality — its inputs are ERA5 reanalysis (~5 day latency) and MAIAC AOD (1–3 day latency).
Outside the training range in places. Every CPCB monitor is urban, so rural cells sit beyond anything the model was trained on. Those are drawn hatched: the model returns its boundary estimate there and the interval was never validated for them.

National AQI band (PM2.5)

Each cell is the model's own 1 km prediction, from that cell's AOD, meteorology, vegetation and land cover — not interpolated from stations. Validated against the monitors: the cell containing a station predicts what the station predicts (correlation 0.990, median difference −0.11 µg/m³).

Every estimate carries a 90% prediction interval, calibrated on out-of-fold errors at stations the model never trained on. Hover any cell to see it.

Red hatching marks cells whose features fall outside the range the model was trained on. Every CPCB monitor is urban, so rural cells are extrapolation by construction: the model returns its boundary estimate and the interval's coverage was never measured there. Grey hatching marks cells more than 10.6 km from any station, a larger gap than validation ever spanned. Distance itself is context only — it was tested against prediction error and found unpredictive.
